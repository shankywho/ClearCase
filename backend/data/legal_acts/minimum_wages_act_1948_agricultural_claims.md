# Minimum Wages Act, 1948
## Provisions on Agricultural Employment, Wage Rates, Claims and Adjudication

### Act Information
- **Act Title**: Minimum Wages Act, 1948 (Central Act No. 11 of 1948)
- **Jurisdiction**: Republic of India (Applicable across all States, including notified agricultural employments)
- **Subject Matter**: Statutory minimum wages for farm labor, casual daily workers, tractor operators, timely disbursement, penalties for delayed or reduced payment, claims adjudication before the Labor Authority / Village Panchayat.

---

### Section 3: Fixing of Minimum Rates of Wages
1. The appropriate Government shall fix the minimum rates of wages payable to employees employed in scheduled employments, including agricultural operations such as cultivation, tillage, sowing, harvesting, threshing, and handling of livestock.
2. The minimum rates of wages may be fixed by the hour, by the day, by the month, or on a piece-work basis (e.g. per bigha harvested or per bundle threshed).
3. The minimum wage shall consist of a basic rate of wage and a cost of living allowance (Variable Dearness Allowance), and in agricultural labor, may include customary grain or fodder concessions where officially notified.

---

### Section 11: Payment of Minimum Wages in Cash or Kind
1. Minimum wages payable under this Act shall be paid in cash currency.
2. Where it has been the custom to pay wages wholly or partly in kind (such as seasonal food grains, wheat, paddy, or cooked food), the State Government may authorize the payment of minimum wages either wholly or partly in kind, provided that the cash valuation of such grain or food shall be computed at notified market rates.
3. No employer or agricultural contractor shall compel any farm worker to accept rotten, damaged, or arbitrarily valued produce in lieu of earned daily cash wages.

---

### Section 12: Obligation to Pay Minimum Wages Without Deductions
1. Where in respect of any scheduled employment a notification has been issued fixing minimum rates of wages, the employer shall pay to every employee engaged in that employment wages at a rate not less than the notified minimum rate without any deductions, except as authorized by law.
2. Any agreement, contract, or verbal understanding whereby an employee agrees to accept less than the statutory minimum wage, or agrees to forfeit balance wages due to customary debt or bonded obligation, shall be null, void, and unenforceable.

---

### Section 15: Wages of Worker Working Less Than Normal Working Day
1. If an employee whose minimum rate of wages has been fixed by the day works on any day on which he was employed for a period less than the requisite number of hours constituting a normal working day, he shall, unless his failure to work was caused by his unwillingness to work and not by the omission of the employer to provide him with work, be entitled to receive wages in respect of work done on that day as if he had worked for a full normal working day.

---

### Section 20: Claims Arising Out of Payment of Less Than Minimum Wages
1. The appropriate Government may appoint any Commissioner for Workmen's Compensation, Officer of the Labor Department, or presiding officer of a Civil Court, or designate the Gram Panchayat / Village Labor Conciliation Officer as the Authority to hear and decide claims arising out of payment of less than the minimum rates of wages or delayed payment of remuneration.
2. Where an employee has any claim arising out of payment of less than the minimum rates of wages, or delayed payment of wages for agricultural labor, the employee himself, or any legal practitioner, or an official of a registered trade union, or an authorized local mediator on his behalf, may apply to the Authority for a direction under sub-section (3).
3. When any application under sub-section (2) is entertained, the Authority shall hear the applicant and the employer, or give them an opportunity of being heard, and after such further inquiry as it considers necessary, may direct:
   - The payment to the employee of the amount by which the minimum wages payable exceed the amount actually paid;
   - Together with the payment of such compensation as the Authority may think fit, not exceeding ten times the amount of such excess or balance due;
   - In any other case, payment of the wage arrears together with compensation up to twenty-five rupees for each day of willful delay.
4. The amount directed to be paid under this section may be recovered as if it were a fine imposed by a Magistrate.

---

### Section 22: Penalties for Non-Payment of Minimum Wages
1. Any employer who pays to any employee less than the minimum rates of wages fixed for that employee's class of work, or who unlawfully withholds earned wages for farm labor, shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to six months, or with fine which may extend to five hundred rupees, or with both.
2. Voluntary settlement and payment of outstanding wages with interest or mutually agreed ex-gratia compensation before an informal mediation body or Lok Adalat may be taken into consideration by the Authority to discharge the complaint amicably.
