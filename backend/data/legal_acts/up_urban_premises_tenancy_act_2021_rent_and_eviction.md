# Uttar Pradesh Regulation of Urban Premises Tenancy Act, 2021 & Model Tenancy Provisions
## Commercial Shop Leases, Tenancy Agreements, Rent Revision, Security Deposit and Eviction Protection

### Act Information
- **Act Title**: Uttar Pradesh Regulation of Urban Premises Tenancy Act, 2021 (U.P. Act No. 16 of 2021) / Model Tenancy Framework
- **Jurisdiction**: State of Uttar Pradesh (Applicable to urban premises, rural market shops, godowns, and rural bazaar commercial units)
- **Subject Matter**: Tenancy agreements for village shops and stalls, permissible rent revisions, security deposit caps, landlord-tenant obligations, protection against arbitrary eviction, dispute resolution through Rent Authority / local Conciliator.

---

### Section 4: Tenancy Agreement and Registration
1. No person shall let out or take on rent any premises, including a retail shop, commercial godown, or workshop, except by an agreement in writing.
2. The agreement shall specify the agreed monthly rent, the tenancy duration, the agreed purpose of letting, and the terms of revision.
3. Where a tenancy has subsisted on a verbal or informal basis in rural markets or mandi areas, the parties shall execute a mutual agreement acknowledging the existing tenure, rent paid, and security deposit held.

---

### Section 8: Rent Payable and Prohibition of Arbitrary Increases
1. The rent payable in respect of any shop or commercial premises shall be the rent agreed upon between the landlord and the tenant.
2. No landlord shall unilaterally inflate or hike the rent during the agreed lease period, except in accordance with the terms of the tenancy agreement or with the mutual consent of the tenant.
3. Where no revision percentage is specified in an informal agreement, any annual increment shall not exceed five percent (5%) for residential premises and seven percent (7%) for commercial shops or stalls, unless substantial additions or structural improvements have been carried out by the landlord with the tenant's prior consent.

---

### Section 10: Restriction on Security Deposit
1. The security deposit to be paid by the tenant in advance shall not exceed:
   - In case of residential premises, an amount equal to two months' rent;
   - In case of non-residential or commercial shops, an amount equal to a maximum of six months' rent.
2. The security deposit shall be refunded to the tenant by the landlord within thirty days of the tenant vacating the premises and handing over peaceful possession, after deducting any legitimate unpaid rent or verified utility dues.
3. The landlord shall not withhold the security deposit on flimsy or unverified grounds of normal wear and tear.

---

### Section 13: Maintenance of Premises and Essential Services
1. Both landlord and tenant shall be responsible for the maintenance and repairs of the premises as agreed. In default of agreement, the landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs (roof, external walls, drainage), and the tenant shall be responsible for day-to-day maintenance (minor fixtures, internal wiring).
2. No landlord shall, either by himself or through any person, cut off or withhold any essential supply or service (such as electricity, drinking water, approach passage, or drainage) enjoyed by the tenant in respect of the shop or premises.
3. If a landlord cuts off any essential service to force the tenant out, the local Rent Authority or Panchayat Conciliator may order immediate restoration of the service and impose compensation on the landlord.

---

### Section 21: Protection Against Unlawful Eviction and Recovery of Possession
1. No landlord shall recover possession of any shop or commercial premises from a tenant except on an order of the Rent Authority or competent court, upon one of the following grounds:
   - Tenant has failed to pay agreed rent for two consecutive months despite notice;
   - Tenant has parted with possession or sub-let the shop without landlord's consent;
   - Tenant has caused severe structural damage or used the shop for illegal or hazardous activities;
   - The landlord bona fide requires the premises for his own immediate commercial or residential occupation.
2. A landlord wishing to terminate a periodic tenancy must provide at least thirty days' prior written notice to the tenant, affording reasonable time to relocate stock and clear accounts.
3. Forceful eviction, changing locks, throwing out goods, or physical intimidation without due process of law is strictly prohibited and entitles the tenant to an order of restitution and damages.
